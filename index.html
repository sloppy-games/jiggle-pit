Who beats what

Block stops slaps, uppercuts, projectiles and most specials, but every hit drains the guard bar. When it empties, the shield shatters and you are stunned.

Sweep is low: it goes under a block and trips. Jump over it.

Shove crushes guard and pushes hardest. Use it at the edge.

Grab bounces off a healthy guard but crushes a weak one.

Parry: tap block right before a hit lands to bounce the attacker off.

Combos & finishers

Signature combo: slap, slap, uppercut. Every wrestler has their own flashy string. It can be blocked.

Juggle: an uppercut launches. Slap them on the way down.

Skull pop: uppercut someone below 15% health and their head comes off.

Limbs only come off from specials.

Fatality: win the deciding round, then press your special while they stagger.
